# PDF Tools v0.9.1: More Exact Shannon Type-3 Recovery

PDF Tools `v0.9.1` is a conservative extraction-quality update for difficult
born-digital PDFs. It recovers 59 additional exact characters from nine
qualified legacy Computer Modern Type-3 raster groups in Claude Shannon's
55-page 1948 paper, *A Mathematical Theory of Communication*.

## What improved

The new batch recovers 26 commas, 13 slashes, 9 rho characters, 6 periods,
3 pi characters, 1 greater-or-equal character, and 1 square root. Each
replacement still requires an unambiguous raw-font link, the official Computer
Modern character position, an exact target glyph fingerprint, two exact
companion-glyph fingerprints, matching font metrics, and complete text/operator
alignment. Missing or ambiguous evidence remains unchanged.

The full Type-3 census now strictly recovers 1,111 of 1,240 officially named
occurrences in the Shannon paper, up from 1,052 in v0.9.0. The 129 remaining
occurrences are explicitly unresolved: 64 minuses without two qualified
companion glyphs, 60 alphas whose source controls are collapsed into whitespace
by PDF.js, and 5 commas without two qualified companion glyphs.

## Install

Claude Desktop users can download and open `pdf-toolkit-mcp.mcpb`. Cursor and
other local stdio MCP hosts can use `pdf-toolkit-mcp.zip` or the checked-out
`server/index.js`.

## Honest limits

PDF Tools does not perform OCR, decode every Type-3 font, reconstruct arbitrary
formulas, infer ambiguous merged tables, or promise paper-wide visual fidelity.
It performs conservative source-text transcription and reports unsupported or
incomplete coverage rather than guessing.
